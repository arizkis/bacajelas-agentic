This page is not BacaJelas — and that's the point.

BacaJelas lives at bacajelas-agentic.web.app. This porch lives at a different origin. On the open agentic web, that's no obstacle: BacaJelas publishes its agent network as public files, so this page can — with your permission — fetch that network, check it for safety, and run its translator right here.

No account. No key on this page at all (there is literally nothing here to steal or leak). Two plain file downloads, one safety check, and Alih the translator does his work in your browser.
